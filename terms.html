Barn Dressage Plus

  • What it unlocks: every test in the app beyond the free first test, in all four modes. Free riding, the first test, customisation and Game Center are free without it.
  • Length and price: one month or one year, auto-renewing. The price is shown on the subscription screen in your local currency before you buy.
  • Payment: charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Renewal: renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; the account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • Managing and cancelling: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Restoring: "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen brings an active subscription to another device using the same Apple ID.
