Barn Dressage Plus subscription

  • What it unlocks: every FEI dressage test in the app beyond the free first test, in all four modes. Free riding, the first test, customisation, Game Center leaderboards and achievements are free without it.
  • Length and price: one month, auto-renewing. The price is shown on the subscription screen in your local currency before you buy.
  • Payment: charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Renewal: the subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Managing and cancelling: in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Restoring: "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen brings an active subscription to another device using the same Apple ID.

Game scores

Marks and scores in the app are game scores for training and fun. They are not official dressage results. Barn Dressage is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FEI.
