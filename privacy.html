Privacy Policy

Barn Dressage · thebarnapp · Effective 18 September 2026

In short: Barn Dressage has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store, transmit or sell personal data. Your horse, settings and scores stay on your device. Game Center and purchases are handled by Apple.

Data we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number or contact list is collected.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
  • No location, camera, microphone or photo access is requested.
  • The app sends no data to servers operated by us or by third parties. We operate no servers for this app.

Data stored on your device

Saved locally so the app remembers it: your settings and language, your horse and rider and the horse's name, your best marks and game scores, which tests you have watched, learned and finished, your daily plays, and the names you typed when recommending the game to a friend (only so each friend counts once). None of it leaves your device through the app; all of it is removed when you delete the app.

Motion sensors

If you choose tilt steering, the app reads the motion sensors to steer the horse. The readings are used on the device in real time and are not stored or transmitted.

Purchases

The Barn Dressage Plus subscription is sold and processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active on your Apple ID.

Sharing

"Recommend to a friend" opens the system share sheet with a short text and an App Store link. The app does not send the message itself and does not learn who received it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. It has no advertising, no chat and no links to social networks.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your game scores and achievements are submitted to Game Center under your Game Center profile. Game Center is operated by Apple and governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. If you are not signed in, nothing is submitted.
